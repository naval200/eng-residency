Rishikesh Learning Residency

A month to learn something difficult, with good people around you.

A self-directed learning residency in Rishikesh for people between jobs and others taking a deliberate learning break. Choose what to study, make a plan, and work on it alongside people doing the same.

What it is

A 30-day self-directed learning residency. It is meant especially for people between jobs who want to use the gap deliberately rather than drift through it.

You choose your focus — AI skills, backend or distributed systems, full-stack or frontend work, an online course, or a project you want to finish. You use your own courses and resources. The residency does not provide a proprietary course library.

You bring the goal and learning material. We bring a place to focus, a community that helps you keep going, and a few ways to spend time outside the desk. Peers and the host check in on progress; you own the work.

  • Not a bootcamp
  • Not job placement
  • Not a fixed curriculum

How it works

Three simple steps. No daily lectures, grades, or rigid timetable.

  1. Create your track.

    Use studycoach.diy to turn a learning goal into a concrete plan. Bring your own books, docs, courses, and projects.

  2. Work alongside peers.

    Residents and the host check in on progress, share ideas, and help each other stay accountable. Expect at least 20 hours of focused learning or building per week.

  3. Use the month well.

    Make progress on a course or project while living in Rishikesh. Optional activities exist if you want them — not a compulsory timetable. See an example public track: ai-study on GitHub.

Optional activities

Kayaking, yoga, spiritual study — or none of these. They are extras, not a mandatory package, and are not included in the core residency price.

  • Kayaking

    We plan to take kayaking slowly. The working idea is a 7-day course spread over about 10 days on weekdays — roughly two practice days, then a break, and so on — so it sits alongside focused learning rather than crowding the month.

    Early sessions stay at NIM beach, with the focus on paddling and swimming in the river. On the last day we may go downstream on easy grade-1 rapids. A working estimate is still around ₹1,000 per session; because this is a multi-day arrangement, the per-session cost may come down once we confirm with an operator. This is being explored; details subject to operator and safety confirmation — not booked or guaranteed.

    If you want something denser, you can also look at a more serious multi-day course such as the ones from Team4Adventure (for example a 4-day course). Since you are here for a month, the slower residency rhythm is usually the better fit — Team4Adventure is a useful local reference, not a confirmation of our exact deal.

  • Yoga

    Drop-in classes are available locally — vinyasa, hatha, aerial, and other forms depending on the studio. Typical drop-in prices run about ₹300, ₹500, or ₹700 per class. Sessions are optional; you pay the studio directly unless a group arrangement is confirmed.

  • Kirtan / spiritual learning

    We are exploring local options. Working estimates are ₹5,000 for seven days or ₹20,000 for a month before the coordination fee; rates, dates, and providers need confirmation. Ask us if you are interested.

Where

The residency is planned for Rishikesh, Uttarakhand. Tapovan is the initial area being explored; an exact venue is not booked yet.

Tentative timing is a winter / early-spring month: December (cold), January (cold), or February (nicer). Exact cohort dates are still open — tell us which window works for you when you express interest.

The appeal is a mix of the Ganga, hills, cafes, kayaking, yoga, and places to explore spiritual subjects — a setting where focused work can sit beside a healthier daily rhythm.

FAQ

Is this a course?
No. It is self-directed; participants use their own learning resources.
Do I need to be between jobs?
That is the main audience, but anyone with the time and commitment may express interest.
Is there a fixed curriculum?
No. Each participant creates a learning track and goal.
Are yoga and kayaking included?
They are optional extras and depend on final local arrangements.
Is the price final?
Not yet. The itemized final price and cancellation terms will be shared before anyone commits.
Will this help me get a job?
We do not promise placement. The aim is to make focused progress on skills or a project.
