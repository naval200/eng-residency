# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step), chosen for a tiny maintainable v1 that hosts anywhere.

## Users

Primary: software engineers and technology professionals between jobs who want a deliberate month to learn something difficult. Also: people taking a planned break or focusing on a project. They need focus, peers, accountability, and a place — not a curriculum or job placement.

## Product Purpose

Explain the **Rishikesh Learning Residency** (working name): a 30-day self-directed learning residency in Rishikesh. Collect interest via a thoughtful form so the host can follow up on WhatsApp for a short discussion. Success: visitors understand the offer in under a minute and a few strong interest submissions arrive.

## Positioning

Participants bring their own goals and learning materials. The residency provides place, community, check-ins, coordination, and optional local experiences. **Not** a bootcamp, job-placement service, fixed curriculum, formal yoga retreat, or workation package. No proprietary course library; Studycoach.diy helps people plan their own track.

## Constraints

- Do not invent cohort dates, venue address, testimonials, alumni counts, partnerships, contact email, or final prices.
- Pricing and optional activities are working estimates; disclose the 15% coordination fee; mark unconfirmed arrangements.
- No online payments on v1.
- No public WhatsApp number; soft follow-up mention only.
- English only for v1.
- Do not store personal form data in localStorage; Formspree when configured, otherwise honest “nothing was sent.”
- Never claim form success when the endpoint is unset.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: calm, direct, human, practical — not corporate or SaaS buzzwordy.
- Working name: Rishikesh Learning Residency.
- Host: Naval Saini (first-person About); links to CV, ai-study GitHub, studycoach.diy.
- Visual brief (binding): calm, tactile, editorial, outdoors — river water, stone, forest, warm paper; not purple AI gradients or card-heavy SaaS landing patterns.

## Accessibility

Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, adequate contrast, form labels, `prefers-reduced-motion` support.

## Open Decisions

- Formspree endpoint (owner pastes into `js/config.js` before live submissions).
- Exact venue, dates, and supplier quotes (TBC; site must say so).
- Hosting target (any static host).
